qwerty-abc-piano — embedding in a native WebView

How a mobile app can host the piano in a WebView: a native iOS/Android app loads a page like this one, drives the widget through a global handle, and receives its events over a JavaScript channel (it can also forward MIDI into the page). Here the widget is mounted, exposed on window.__qap, and its notifications are forwarded through a simulated JS channel. Tap the on-screen piano (no keyboard needed) to play.

Host (native app) → JS — runJavaScript(...)

Native buttons drive chord entry without a physical keyboard, doing the same job as the widget's own Start/End chord button.
